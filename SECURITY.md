You should really allow people to submit bugs without pushing them back to the local vendor... who claims they must pay up for an additional support subscription just for submitting bugs.

Allowing everyone, regardless of paid subscription, to submit bugs is essential for the quality progress of opensource projects.

Look at big projects like Redhat and Ubuntu : the majority of their bugfixes come through bugs reported via respectively bugzilla and launchpad by free using students and devops.

Your current policy is not just frustrating for free users but holds you back from improving your product.
